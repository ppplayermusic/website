PPPlayer is a free, open-source music player designed to get out of your way and let you listen. It connects to powerful metadata APIs and playback engines to provide a complete streaming experience, but without the baggage of modern streaming services.

## No Account. No Subscriptions. No Ads.

With most music apps, you have to create an account, log in, manage a subscription, and deal with advertisements. With PPPlayer, you just open the app and search for what you want to hear. Your library, favorites, and queue are stored locally on your device.

## Why Open Source?

Being open source under the MIT license means anyone can see how PPPlayer works, verify what it does, and contribute to its development. The community drives the features. If there is a bug, anyone can submit a fix. If there is a missing feature, anyone can build it.

## Native by Design

PPPlayer isn't just a website wrapped in a window. It uses native UI components, context menus, and hardware acceleration on macOS, Windows, and Linux to ensure a beautiful, performant experience.
