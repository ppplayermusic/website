PPPlayer is built to feel right at home on macOS. With native context menus, smooth window resizing, and integration with the macOS media controller, it offers a premium experience without the premium price tag.

![PPPlayer running natively on macOS](/images/blog/ppplayer-mac-app.png)

## Installation

1. Visit [ppplayer.com/download](https://ppplayer.com/download) and download the macOS version.
2. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
3. Drag the PPPlayer app icon into your **Applications** folder.
4. Launch PPPlayer from Launchpad or Spotlight.

Since our releases are officially signed and notarized by Apple, you won't see any scary "unidentified developer" warnings.

## Native Integrations

PPPlayer hooks directly into macOS:

- **Media Keys:** Use your keyboard's play, pause, and skip keys to control playback, even when PPPlayer is in the background.
- **Control Center:** The currently playing track appears in the macOS Control Center and lock screen.
- **Context Menus:** Right-click anywhere—on a track, artist, or album—to see native context menus to add to queue, go to artist, and more.

## Gapless Background Playback

macOS has a feature called "App Nap" that slows down apps when they are minimized or hidden to save battery. PPPlayer is specifically engineered to prevent App Nap from interrupting your music, ensuring gapless transitions between songs in your queue even when the app is completely hidden.
