PPPlayer keeps things simple. Without accounts or cloud syncing, your music management happens entirely on your device. Here is how to use the core features.

## Global Search

The search bar is your gateway to music. When you search, PPPlayer queries comprehensive music metadata APIs to instantly return:

- **Top Results:** The most relevant matches.
- **Tracks:** Individual songs you can play immediately.
- **Artists:** Artist profiles with their discography.
- **Albums:** Full album tracklists.

Just type what you're looking for and hit Enter.

## Managing Your Favorites

When you find an album, track, or artist you love, click the **Heart** icon. 

- Favorited items are saved instantly to your local device.
- You can access them anytime from the **Your Library** section in the sidebar.
- Because they are saved locally, there is no loading time when you open your library.

## Controlling the Queue

The Queue in PPPlayer is designed to be completely transparent and easily manageable.

- **Play Next:** Right-click any track and select "Play Next" to insert it immediately after the current song.
- **Add to Queue:** Select "Add to Queue" to append it to the very end of your current list.
- **Reorder:** Open the Queue view (the list icon next to the volume controls) and simply drag and drop tracks to rearrange them.

PPPlayer also features context-aware autoplay. If you reach the end of your queue, the app will intelligently find related tracks and keep the music going.
