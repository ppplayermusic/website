PPPlayer provides a native, beautiful experience on Android. Because it is independent and open-source, we distribute the Android APK directly on our website and GitHub releases page.

## Downloading the App

To install PPPlayer on your Android device:

1. Go to the [PPPlayer Download Page](https://ppplayer.com/download).
2. Tap the **Android** download button. This will download the latest APK file.
3. Open the downloaded APK file.

*Note: Google Play distribution is planned for the future, but currently, direct download is the only official way to install the app on Android.*

## Allowing Unknown Sources

If this is your first time installing an app from outside the Google Play Store, Android will ask you to allow installations from your browser or file manager.

1. When the prompt appears, tap **Settings**.
2. Toggle the switch for **Allow from this source**.
3. Tap the back arrow and select **Install**.

## Updating PPPlayer

When a new version is released, you can update simply by downloading the new APK from our website and opening it. Android will recognize the existing installation and prompt you to update without losing any of your local favorites, history, or settings.
